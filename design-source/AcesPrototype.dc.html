Association of Computer
Engineering Students

ACES is the official student body representing every Computer Engineering student at KNUST — a platform to connect, learn and grow together.

We run technical workshops, career programs, mentorship, industrial visits and community service, and we work to bridge the gap between classroom learning and real-world application.

Mission
To equip Computer Engineering students with the skills, exposure and network they need to lead in technology.
Vision
A community where innovation thrives, ideas are shared and every member is empowered to succeed.
